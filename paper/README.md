McGaughey, T. and Peters, P.A. (2026). A Hybrid Dasymetric Method for Hexagonal Population Density Mapping in Canada: Reconciling Road-Based and Address-Based Allocation at National Scale. *Preprints.org*. https://doi.org/10.20944/preprints202609.2636.v1

The preprint is a version that has not been peer reviewed. The layers in release 2.0.0 extend the method to resolutions 6 and 7 and add the improvements listed in `CHANGELOG.md`. When the peer-reviewed article is published, its citation will be added here and to `CITATION.cff`. The accepted manuscript will be added only where the publisher's sharing policy permits it.
